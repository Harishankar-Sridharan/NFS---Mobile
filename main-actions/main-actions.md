# Dashboard

The dashboard is the home page of the NFS Mobile app. It opens after you log in and shows your day at a glance: your planned interventions, the progress of the day and shortcuts to find or resume an intervention.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-dashboard-home.png" alt="NFS Mobile dashboard" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The dashboard contains the following areas, from top to bottom:

* [Header](main-actions.md#header)
* [Shortcuts](main-actions.md#shortcuts)
* [Day summary](main-actions.md#day-summary)
* [Interventions](main-actions.md#interventions)
* [Navigation bar](main-actions.md#navigation-bar)

### Header

The header at the top of the screen shows:

* Your photo and name.
* A synchronization icon with a time next to it, shown in green.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-dashboard-header.png" alt="Dashboard header and shortcuts" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Shortcuts

Two shortcuts are displayed under the header:

* **Search** – find an intervention.
* **In progress** – list the interventions that are in progress.

#### Search

Use **Search** to find an intervention.

1. On the dashboard, tap **Search**.
2. In the **Intervention** page, tap the search field and enter your search.

You can find an intervention by:

* Intervention number
* Date
* Address
* Name

To search by date, tap the calendar icon at the right of the search field.

To go back to your schedule, tap **Going to my calendar** at the bottom of the screen. To return to the dashboard, tap the back arrow at the top left.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-dashboard-search.png" alt="Intervention search page" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### In progress

Use **In progress** to see your ongoing interventions.

1. On the dashboard, tap **In progress**.
2. The **My ongoing interventions** page lists each intervention that is in progress.

Each intervention is displayed in a card that shows:

* How long ago the intervention started, for example **Since 122h 34min**.
* The customer name, for example **BUFFALO GRILL**.
* The address.
* The type of intervention, for example **Controle 2h**.
* The planned duration and the date and time.

To return to the dashboard, tap the back arrow at the top left.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-dashboard-in-progress.png" alt="My ongoing interventions page" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Day summary

The day summary shows the state of the selected day.

* **Today** and the date, for example **2026-10-20**. The dots between them indicate that other days are available.
* **Interventions of the day** – a progress bar with the number of interventions completed out of the number planned for the day, for example **0 / 0**.
* **Route** – the route time for the day.
* **Travel** – the distance to travel, in miles.
* **Intervention** – the time planned for the interventions.

### Interventions

The **Interventions** line shows the number of interventions in your list, displayed in a yellow badge. In the video, the badge shows **5**.

### Navigation bar

The navigation bar at the bottom of the screen lets you move between the main pages of the app. It has five icons, from left to right:

* **Menu** (three horizontal lines)
* **Dashboard** (gauge) – the current page, highlighted in blue
* **Calendar**
* **Contacts** (two people)
* **Settings** (gear)
