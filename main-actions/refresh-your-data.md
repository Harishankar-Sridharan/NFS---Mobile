# Refresh Your Data

Reload your interventions and documents so that you work with the latest information.

## Overview

NFS Mobile keeps a copy of your data on the device. Your planner can change your interventions during the day. Refresh your data to get the latest version.

The dashboard shows the time of the last refresh at the top right of the header, next to the green synchronization icon, for example **14h27**.

## Refresh the dashboard

1. Open the [dashboard](main-actions.md).
2. In the header, check the time next to the synchronization icon.
3. Tap the synchronization icon.
4. Wait while the data is reloaded. The time of the last refresh is updated.

## Refresh the documents of an intervention

On the **Documents** page of an intervention, tap the refresh button at the top right of the page. See [View the Documents of an Intervention](../intervention/documents.md#refresh-the-list).

## Clear the cache

If the data is still wrong after a refresh, clear the cache of the app. See [Clear the cache](settings.md#clear-the-cache).
