# Items and Prices

## Overview

These topics cover the items of an intervention: add and remove them, read their details and their extra fields, record their serial numbers, and calculate their price with the tax.

## Topics

| Topic | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| [Add Items to an Intervention](add-items-to-an-intervention.md) | Record the items that you use or sell during an intervention, and check their price. |
| [View the Details of an Item](view-item-details.md) | Open the product sheet of an item to check its stock, its references and its price. |
| [View and Edit the Extra Fields of an Item](edit-extra-item-fields.md) | See and update the additional information that your company adds to an item, such as a link, a checkbox, a number or a date. |
| [Mandatory Fields and Drop-Down Lists on Items](mandatory-and-list-item-fields.md) | Choose a value in a drop-down list, and fill in the mandatory fields of the items that you used. |
| [Scan the Mandatory Serial Numbers](mandatory-serial-number.md) | Scan the serial number of each item that your company marks as mandatory, before you finish the intervention. |
| [Accept Only Valid Scan Codes](valid-scan-codes.md) | Know which scanned codes the app accepts for an item, and what happens when a code is rejected. |
| [Scan or Enter a Serial Number](scan-a-serial-number.md) | Link a barcode or QR code, or a number you type, to an item of the intervention. |
| [Calculate the Price of the Items](calculate-item-prices.md) | Calculate the price of each item and the total of the intervention, with the tax. |
| [Remove an Item](remove-an-item.md) | Swipe an item to remove it from the intervention. |
