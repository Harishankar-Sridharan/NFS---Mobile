# Reports, Photos and Documents

## Overview

These topics cover everything that you record or read during an intervention: the reports and the survey forms, the photos and the remarks, and the documents that are linked to it. Some of them can be mandatory, and some of them work offline.

## Topics

| Topic | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| [Fill in the Report](report.md) | Fill in the report of an intervention in progress. |
| [Correct Your Text with AI](correct-your-text-with-ai.md) | Correct and improve the text of a field with the AI text correction button, in tasks, remarks and reports. |
| [Add and Delete a Report During an Intervention](add-a-report.md) | Add another report to an intervention, fill it in, and delete it if you added it by mistake. |
| [Complete the Mandatory Survey Reports](mandatory-survey-reports.md) | Fill in the survey reports that your company requires before you can finish an intervention. |
| [Add Files and Signatures to a Survey Report](add-files-to-a-survey-report.md) | Add photos, videos, documents and signatures to the fields of a survey report. |
| [Fill In Survey Forms Offline](fill-in-forms-offline.md) | Open, fill in and update survey forms at any stage of the intervention, even when your device has no network. |
| [Add Photos and Remarks](photos-and-remarks.md) | Add photos and remarks to an intervention in progress. |
| [Draw on a Photo](draw-on-a-photo.md) | Draw, write or add shapes on a photo of an intervention. |
| [Take the Required Photos](required-photos.md) | Take the minimum number of photos that your company requires before you finish an intervention. |
| [View the Documents of an Intervention](documents.md) | Open the files and the reports linked to an intervention, and refresh the list. |
| [Scan a Document](scan-a-document.md) | Scan a paper document with the camera, improve it, and save it as a PDF or a JPEG file. |
