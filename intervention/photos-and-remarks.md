# Add Photos and Remarks

Add photos and remarks to an intervention in progress.

## Overview

You can add photos to document your work and write remarks about the intervention. A number on the **Photos** shortcut shows how many photos the intervention contains.

## Before you begin

* The intervention is in progress. See [Start an Intervention](start-an-intervention.md).
* The app is allowed to use the camera of your device.

## Add a photo

1. On the **Intervention** page, tap **Photos**.
2. The **Photos/Videos** page opens. If there is no photo yet, the message **No photos or videos for the moment** is displayed.
3. Tap the camera button at the bottom of the screen.
4. Take the photo.
5. Check the photo, then tap the check mark to keep it. Tap the red cross to delete it, or the blue arrow to take it again.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-intervention-photos.png" alt="Photos/Videos page" width="300"><figcaption><p>Photos/Videos page</p></figcaption></figure>

The photo appears on the **Photos/Videos** page with a label, for example **After**, and its file name.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-intervention-photo-added.png" alt="Photo added to the intervention" width="300"><figcaption><p>Photo added to the intervention</p></figcaption></figure>

On the **Photos/Videos** page, you can also:

* Tap the middle button at the bottom of the screen to add a photo from your gallery.
* Tap the red bin icon next to a photo to delete it.
* Tap the pencil icon next to a photo to edit it.
* Tap the arrow button to continue.
* Tap the cross at the top right to close the page.

### If the photo is rejected

If the photo is too blurry, the message **Insufficient quality** is displayed at the top of the screen: **The photo you submitted is blurry, of insufficient quality, or does not meet the required criteria.**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-intervention-photo-quality.png" alt="Insufficient quality message" width="300"><figcaption><p>Insufficient quality message</p></figcaption></figure>

1. Tap the retake button, in the middle, to take the photo again.
2. Tap the check mark to keep the new photo.

## Preview a photo

You can open a photo in full screen to check it.

1. On the **Photos/Videos** page, tap a photo.
2. The photo opens in a preview. It is centered in the screen, so its alignment is correct.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-photo-preview-fit.png" alt="Photo preview" width="300"><figcaption><p>Photo preview</p></figcaption></figure>

3. To zoom in or out, pinch the photo with two fingers.
4. To see another part of a zoomed photo, move it with your finger.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-photo-preview-zoom.png" alt="Zoomed photo" width="300"><figcaption><p>Zoomed photo</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-photo-preview-zoom-more.png" alt="Photo zoomed in more" width="300"><figcaption><p>Photo zoomed in more</p></figcaption></figure>

5. To leave the preview, tap the cross at the top right, or the back button of your device.

## Write remarks

1. On the **Intervention** page, tap **Remarks**.
2. In the **Remarks** page, enter your remarks in the text area.
3. To correct the spelling of your text, tap **Correct the text**.
4. To correct and improve your text, tap **Correct and improve the text**.
5. To delete your text, tap **Erase all**.
6. Tap the arrow button at the bottom of the screen to continue.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-intervention-remarks.png" alt="Remarks page" width="300"><figcaption><p>Remarks page</p></figcaption></figure>

## Show or hide the Remarks tab

Your company can hide the **Remarks** shortcut. Then you cannot write remarks, and the shortcut is not displayed once the intervention is started.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-remarks-hidden.png" alt="Intervention without the Remarks shortcut" width="300"><figcaption><p>Intervention without the Remarks shortcut</p></figcaption></figure>

When the shortcut is displayed, tap **Remarks** and write your text. The remarks are sent with the intervention and appear in the visit report in NFS.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-remarks-visible.png" alt="Intervention with the Remarks shortcut" width="300"><figcaption><p>Intervention with the Remarks shortcut</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/nfs-mobile-remarks-page.png" alt="Remarks page" width="300"><figcaption><p>Remarks page</p></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If your company hides the tab after you wrote remarks, the text you wrote is not sent to the back office. Log out and log in again after a change, to load it.
{% endhint %}

The administrator controls this tab. See [Remarks Tab Setup](../configuration/remarks-tab-setup.md).

## Photos required by your company

If your company requires a minimum number of photos, the **Photos** shortcut shows an alert and you cannot finish the intervention before you reach the minimum. See [Take the Required Photos](required-photos.md).
