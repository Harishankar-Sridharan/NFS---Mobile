# Quick Start

Follow your first intervention from login to the last tap, with a link to the full topic for each step.

## Overview

This page is a short path through the app for a field user. Each step is one tap or one action, and it links to the topic that explains it in detail. Your company may switch some steps on or off, for example the photos or the signature, so you may not see every step.

## Before you begin

* You have the login details that your company gave you.
* The NFS Mobile app is installed on your device.

## Your first intervention

1. **Sign in.** Open the app and enter your login details. See [Login](app-launch/app-launch.md).
2. **Check your day.** The dashboard opens. It shows your day, your route and your interventions. See [Dashboard](main-actions/main-actions.md).
3. **Find your intervention.** Tap the **Calendar** icon, select the day, and tap the intervention. See [Calendar](main-actions/calendar.md).
4. **Accept it.** If the status is **Waiting**, tap **Accept**. See [Accept an intervention](main-actions/calendar.md#accept-an-intervention).
5. **Start it.** Tap **Begin**, then **Confirm**. The status changes to **In progress** and a timer starts. See [Start an Intervention](intervention/start-an-intervention.md).
6. **Do the work.** Use the shortcuts at the top of the **Intervention** page:
   * **Tasks**: complete the tasks. See [Scan a Task Code](intervention/scan-a-task-code.md).
   * **Reports**: fill in the report. See [Fill in the Report](intervention/report.md).
   * **Photos** and **Remarks**: add photos and remarks. See [Add Photos and Remarks](intervention/photos-and-remarks.md).
   * **Items**: record the items that you used, and calculate the price. See [Add Items to an Intervention](intervention/add-items-to-an-intervention.md).
7. **Have it signed.** Tap **Finish**, then choose **By hand** or **Securely**. See [Sign an Intervention](intervention/sign-an-intervention/README.md).
8. **Finish it.** Confirm that you complete the intervention. The app sends your data to NFS. See [Finish an Intervention](intervention/finish-an-intervention.md).
9. **Move on.** Open the next intervention from the calendar or from the dashboard.

## If something does not go as planned

| Situation | What to do |
| --------- | ---------- |
| You cannot carry out the intervention | [Abandon an Intervention](intervention/abandon-an-intervention.md). If you started it by mistake, [Cancel an Intervention](intervention/cancel-an-intervention.md). |
| You need to go back to an abandoned intervention | [Restart an Abandoned Intervention](intervention/restart-an-abandoned-intervention.md) |
| The customer is not available at that time | [Reschedule an Intervention](intervention/reschedule-an-intervention.md) |
| You have no network | The app keeps your work and sends it later. See [Fill In Survey Forms Offline](intervention/fill-in-forms-offline.md) and [Refresh Your Data](main-actions/refresh-your-data.md). |
| The app shows a message that you do not understand | [Messages and Troubleshooting](reference/messages-and-troubleshooting.md) |

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Refresh your data**: If your agenda does not show a change that your planner made, refresh your data. See [Refresh Your Data](main-actions/refresh-your-data.md).
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Look at the shortcuts**: A number on a shortcut of the **Intervention** page shows how many items it contains. A yellow mark on the **Photos** shortcut means that your company requires photos. See [Take the Required Photos](intervention/required-photos.md).
{% endhint %}
