# What's New

See the features that were added to NFS Mobile, from the newest to the oldest, with a link to each topic.

## Overview

Each entry below is a sprint review in which the features were demonstrated. The dates are the dates of the demonstrations. They are not the release dates of the app, so check the version of your app in the **Menu**.

The topics that are not listed here, for example the login, the dashboard, the calendar, the sites and the working hours, describe the base features of the app.

## 20 November 2025

* [Clustered Markers on the Maps](main-actions/map-clustering.md): nearby interventions and sites are grouped in numbered circles on the maps.
* [Choose the Unavailability Type by Group](menu/unavailabilities/choose-the-unavailability-type-by-group.md): pick a group first, then the type. Planned for the next version of the app. Administrators: see [Unavailability Groups Setup](configuration/unavailability-groups-setup.md).

## 3 July 2025

* [Change My Avatar](menu/change-my-avatar.md): take a photo or choose an image for your avatar, also offline.
* [Calculate the Price of the Items](intervention/calculate-item-prices.md): the price is calculated when you tap the calculator, with the tax of each item. Administrators: see [Price Calculation Setup](configuration/price-calculation-setup.md).

## 19 June 2025

* [Grouped Interventions](main-actions/grouped-interventions.md): the interventions of the same customer on the same day are grouped, and you can sign once for the group.
* [Scan a Task Code](intervention/scan-a-task-code.md): a task can have no code yet, and the first scan records it. Administrators: see [Scan Task Setup](configuration/scan-task-setup.md).
* [Quick Actions on an Intervention](main-actions/long-press-an-intervention.md): call the contact, get directions or accept with a long press.
* [Open an Intervention with a Scan](main-actions/open-an-intervention-with-a-scan.md): scan a code to open an intervention. Administrators: see [Intervention Scan Code Setup](configuration/intervention-scan-code-setup.md).
* [Hotel Nights in the Calendar](main-actions/calendar-hotel-nights.md): hotel nights are shown apart from the rest periods. Administrators: see [Hotel Nights Setup](configuration/hotel-nights-setup.md).
* [Add and Delete a Report During an Intervention](intervention/add-a-report.md): the reports that you can open depend on the type and the group of the intervention.

## 5 June 2025

* [Add and Delete a Report During an Intervention](intervention/add-a-report.md): add another report to an intervention, and delete a report that you added.

## 22 May 2025

* [Complete the Mandatory Survey Reports](intervention/mandatory-survey-reports.md): the app marks the mandatory survey reports and blocks the end of the intervention until they are completed. Administrators: see [Mandatory Reports Setup](configuration/mandatory-reports-setup.md).
* [Unavailabilities](menu/unavailabilities/README.md): declare an unavailability for a day in the past, and see the past days in the agenda. Administrators: see [Unavailability Days Setup](configuration/unavailability-days-setup.md).

## 24 April 2025

* [Fill In Survey Forms Offline](intervention/fill-in-forms-offline.md): open and complete the survey forms before the start, and without a network.
* [Mandatory Fields and Drop-Down Lists on Items](intervention/mandatory-and-list-item-fields.md): choose a value in a list, and fill in the mandatory fields of the items that you used.

## 10 April 2025

* [View and Edit the Extra Fields of an Item](intervention/edit-extra-item-fields.md): see and update the extra fields of an item. Administrators: see [Extra Item Fields Setup](configuration/extra-item-fields-setup.md).
* [Agenda Position and Saved Day](main-actions/calendar-agenda-position.md): the agenda centers the current time and returns to the day that you were viewing.
* [Calendar Map Marker Colors](main-actions/calendar-map-marker-colors.md): the color of a marker shows the status of the intervention.

## 27 March 2025

* [Change the Measurement Unit](main-actions/change-the-measurement-unit.md): show the distances in kilometers or in miles. Administrators: see [Measurement Unit Setup](configuration/measurement-unit-setup.md).
* [Fill In the Required Fields for Electronic Signature](intervention/sign-an-intervention/required-fields-for-electronic-signature.md): the app checks the contact and technician details before the secure signature.
* [Preview a photo](intervention/photos-and-remarks.md#preview-a-photo): open a photo in full screen, then zoom and move it.

## 13 March 2025

* [View Your Ongoing Interventions](main-actions/view-ongoing-interventions.md): the **In progress** tab of the dashboard lists the interventions that you have started.

## 27 February 2025

* [Take the Required Photos](intervention/required-photos.md): the app blocks the end of the intervention until you take the minimum number of photos. Administrators: see [Required Photos Setup](configuration/required-photos-setup.md).
* [Restart an Abandoned Intervention](intervention/restart-an-abandoned-intervention.md): abandoned interventions appear in the agenda and can be started again. Administrators: see [Abandoned Missions Setup](configuration/abandoned-missions-setup.md).
* [Send Your Location Logs](main-actions/send-location-logs.md): send the log file of the position tracking to the back office. Administrators: see [Location Logs Setup](configuration/location-logs-setup.md).
* [Show or hide the Remarks tab](intervention/photos-and-remarks.md#show-or-hide-the-remarks-tab): your administrator can hide the Remarks shortcut. See [Remarks Tab Setup](configuration/remarks-tab-setup.md).

## 13 February 2025

* [Scan or Enter a Serial Number](intervention/scan-a-serial-number.md): scan a barcode or type a serial number for an item. Administrators: see [Serial Number Setup](configuration/serial-number-setup.md).
* [View the Details of an Item](intervention/view-item-details.md): the product sheet was redesigned.
* [Remove an Item](intervention/remove-an-item.md): swipe an item to remove it.
* [Search the catalog](intervention/add-items-to-an-intervention.md#search-the-catalog): search for an item and add it to the intervention.
* [Start an Intervention](intervention/start-an-intervention.md): a warning appears when you already have interventions in progress.

## 30 January 2025

* [Privacy Mode](menu/coworkers/privacy-mode.md) and [View Your Coworkers](menu/coworkers/view-your-coworkers.md).
* [Sign an Intervention](intervention/sign-an-intervention/README.md): sign by hand, sign securely, or decline to sign.
* [Add Items to an Intervention](intervention/add-items-to-an-intervention.md) and the price result.
* [View the Documents of an Intervention](intervention/documents.md) and [Refresh Your Data](main-actions/refresh-your-data.md).
* [Mobile Configuration](configuration/README.md): the application settings, the signature settings, the search around settings and the price calculation setup.
